# Experimental Design | R | Statistical Analysis | Hypothesis Testing

## Project Overview

Can the way a soda is poured or consumed influence how much someone enjoys its taste?

This project investigates whether different pouring methods and straw usage affect perceived soda taste. Using a 3 × 2 factorial experiment with a randomized complete block design (RCBD), we examined how six participants rated Coca-Cola under different serving conditions.

The experiment was designed to determine whether pouring technique, straw usage, or their interaction significantly influenced participants' enjoyment ratings.

Objective: Evaluate whether different methods of pouring and consuming soda result in statistically significant differences in perceived taste.

## Experimental Design
| Factor | Levels |
|---|---|
| **Pouring Method** | Side of cup, center of cup, directly from can |
| **Straw Usage** | Straw, no straw |

Each participant tested all six combinations of these conditions.

## Design Structure

Design: 3 × 2 factorial experiment

Blocking: Randomized complete block design (RCBD)

Participants: 6

Experimental conditions: 6

Total observations: 36

Response variable: Taste enjoyment score (0–10)

Participants served as blocks to account for individual differences in taste preferences. The order of the experimental conditions was randomized for each participant to reduce potential order effects.
